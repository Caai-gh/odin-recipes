Air Fryer Sweet Potato

Description

A tried-and-true method for achieving easy-to-peel sweet potatoes straight out of the air fryer. Just make sure to leave a little time for them to cool, because the temptation will be to bite right in!

Check out kkari.recipe's channel on Instagram for more inspired cooking. I first used this method when I didn't have a freezer, and didn't have a bag of ice handy. Very cold water from the faucet, or water cooled in the fridge will do the trick just as well.

Ingredients

  • Sweet potatoes (as many as you can fit in your air fryer)
  • tsp olive oil
  • enough ice or very cold water to cover the potatoes in a bowl

Steps

  1. Preheat air fryer to 190°C
  2. Rinse potatoes and pat them dry
  3. Cut the ends off of the potatoes
  4. Coat the potatoes very lightly in olive oil to prevent sticking
  5. Place potatoes in preheated air fryer in one layer
  6. Cook for 25 min.
  7. Prepare ice bath (or very cold water bath). Remove potatoes from air fryer with tongs and place them in the water. Let sit for two minutes.
  8. Return the potatoes to the air fryer and cook for an additional 25 minutes.
  9. Before removing, ensure the potatoes are cooked by poking with a fork or knife and ensuring they are soft all the way through.
  10. Carefully remove potatoes from the fryer and lay on a plate or pan. Carefully cut skin away using kitchen scizzors or using a knife and fork.
  11. Sweet potatos are great to eat on their own with a little bit of butter and brown sugar, or with a but of salt and some spices added on. Or use with anoter recipe, such as this sweet potato and bean skillet.
