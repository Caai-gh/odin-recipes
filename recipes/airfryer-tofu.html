Airfryer Tofu

Description

This is my go-to, super easy tofu recipe. For those days I have little energy, when the fridge is full of leftover sides but no main, and for a snack I can whip up and reheat or happily eat cold, I return to this recipe. All you need is a handful of ingredients, an air fryer, and about an hour’s time.

Ingredients

  • 1 block firm tofu
  • 4 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • ½ tbsp garlic powder
  • drizzle of olive oil

Steps

  1. Drain and press tofu for 30min
  2. Cut tofu into 1" cubes
  3. Combine soy sauce, sesame oil, and garlic powder into a marinade, and soak the cubes of tofu in bowl or plastic baggie for 15min while preparing the air fryer.
  4. Brush olive oil to coat air fryer and heat to 190°C.
  5. Drain marinade and fry tofu cubes for 6min; toss; fry for an additional 6min.
  6. Serve solo, in accompanyment of rice or noodles, or incorporate into stir fry.
